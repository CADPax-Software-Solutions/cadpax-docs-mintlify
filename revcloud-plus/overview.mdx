RevCloud Plus is a CADPax Vectorworks plug-in for annotating drawing revisions. It creates revision clouds and labels that store revision information on the object, with tools for managing cloud graphics and coordinating records with a sheet's Title Block Border.

## What you can do

- **Mark changes:** draw a revision cloud from a path around an affected area.
- **Record revision details:** store the revision number or letter, date, author and description.
- **Control presentation:** configure cloud size and variation, revision-label bubbles and graphics classes.
- **Coordinate revision records:** inspect and update Title Block Border revision information from the settings dialog.
- **Review multiple clouds:** use the companion manager when it is included in your build.
